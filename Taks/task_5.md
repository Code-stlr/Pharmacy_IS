# Hardware & Peripherals for YOUR Project · Maximum 2 points

**Purpose:** Turn one real user action from your semester project into a clear, secure, and cost-checked hardware plan.

---

## Submission Rules

* **Format:** Submit ONLY a Microsoft Word (.docx) file in MS Teams.
* **File Name:** `StudentID_Name_Task05.docx`
* **Structure:** Part A is the same for all team members (write all members' names and IDs). Part B must be your own unique work.
* **Language:** Simple English is perfect. Grammar is not graded — your analytical thinking and system reasoning are what matter.

---

## PART A — TEAM: One Shared Hardware Plan (0.5 points)

Work together as a team. Choose ONE key user action in your semester project (for example: if your project is an LMS, the action can be *"a teacher starts an online lecture"* or *"a student logs in using FaceID"*).

1. **USER ACTION:** Describe your chosen user action in 1–2 simple sentences.
2. **HARDWARE MAP:** List at least 4 peripheral devices physically used in this action. Mark each device as: **INPUT**, **OUTPUT**, **STORAGE**, or **BOTH**.
3. **COMPATIBILITY & PORTS:** Specify how these devices connect to each other or to the computer (e.g., USB-C, HDMI, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth). Name one connection or compatibility risk.
4. **TEAM SECURITY RULE:** Write one strict hardware security rule for your process based on the lecture (think about unknown USB drives, data encryption, updates, or port locking).
5. **PLAN B (OPERATIONAL RESILIENCE):** Which device is most likely to fail first (e.g., battery runs out, cable breaks, paper runs out)? Write a short Plan B workflow so the user can finish the action when that device breaks.

---

## PART B — INDIVIDUAL: Total Cost of Ownership Analysis (1.5 points)

Complete this part strictly by yourself. Team members cannot choose the same device. Each student must pick ONE separate peripheral from the Part A list (e.g., Student 1 analyzes the webcam, Student 2 analyzes the external SSD, Student 3 analyzes the microphone).

1. **MY DEVICE CHOICE:** Which specific peripheral from your team's list did you choose to analyze and why is it important for your project?
2. **TOTAL COST OF OWNERSHIP (TCO):** Explain why the price on the box is not the price the company pays. Calculate and list at least 3 hidden costs for your device over 1 year of daily work (e.g., supplies, batteries, repairs, setup time, software licenses, adapters).
3. **ENGINEERING DECISION & WHY:** State one engineering or buying decision you made for this device and explain WHY you made it (use the structure: *"I decided to choose [X] instead of [Y], BECAUSE..."*).
4. **EVIDENCE OF CONTRIBUTION:** Attach a screenshot or photo showing your individual teamwork contribution this week (e.g., a chat screenshot, a piece of a Miro board, or a snippet of code/shared document).
