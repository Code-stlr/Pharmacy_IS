# IT Infrastructure and Peer Security Audit

* **Max Grade:** 2 points (1 point — Group Part, 1 point — Individual Part)
* **Format:** Only one `.docx` file from each student

---

## PART 1. GROUP PROJECT BLOCK (Max — 1 point)

This part must be done together with your team. The text must be exactly the same in the files of all team members.

* **Task:** Imagine that an investor gave your team money for only ONE security solution. You must choose one and explain why you drop the second one.
* **Project Name:** `[Write strictly from your list, for example: Electronic Health Record, Ticket Booking System, Pharmacy IS, etc.]`
* **The Dilemma:** What is more important for your system to install first right now: **Automatic Data Backup** OR **A Full Network Firewall**?
* **Team Decision:** Choose only one (you cannot choose both). Explain why this tool is more important for your specific system. Describe what exact catastrophe will happen to your project if you do not have the second tool.

### Grading Criteria for Part 1:
* **1 point:** You chose strictly one tool. Your explanation connects directly to your project (for example: *“For Electronic Health Record, Backup is critical because losing patient history is a big problem. We can protect the network manually for now.”*).
* **0 points:** You chose both tools, or your text uses copy-paste definitions from the internet without connecting to your project.

---

## PART 2. INDIVIDUAL BLOCK: "Peer Review" (Max — 1 point)

Every student must write this part strictly alone. The text must be 100% unique. If two students from the same team have identical texts in Part 2, both get 0 points.

**Task:** Fill out your personal security engineer card.

* **Item 2.1. My Personal Vote (0.5 points):**
  If you did this project completely alone, what would you choose? If you vote for Firewall, what specific weakness of your system (from the slides: *Stolen password, Phishing, Zero-day*) will you block first? If you choose Backup, what type of malware (from the slides: *Ransomware, Virus, Spyware*) are you saving your system from? Explain for your specific module.

* **Item 2.2. Attack on a Teammate (0.5 points):**
  Write the real name of one teammate. Imagine this teammate made a big mistake in their part of the code or interface. Describe one specific threat that will appear in your system because of their mistake. What security tool from the slides (*MFA* or *User training*) will you use to fix this?

### Grading Criteria for Part 2:
* **0.5 points for Item 2.1:** You showed your personal opinion and logically connected it to the lecture terms (weaknesses or malware) regarding your project.
* **0.5 points for Item 2.2:** You wrote the real name of your teammate, described a realistic mistake scenario for your system, and chose the correct tool (MFA or User training).
